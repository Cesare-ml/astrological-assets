## Required review

1. Confirm which source fonts, glyphs or vector drawings were actually used to produce the committed raster images, if any.
2. Review the source font's original license agreement or a documented permission from its rightsholder. Do not infer permission from a download button or a marketplace label.
3. Determine whether the final raster output can be redistributed under the proposed artwork license; distinguish font software redistribution from output graphics, and seek qualified advice where restrictions are unclear.
4. Record specific clearance for the relevant asset families and exact SHA-256 files in the public rights register; if uncertain, exclude or independently redraw the affected assets in a clean-history public export.

This is a provenance investigation, not legal advice. Neither the CPAL code license nor the owner's authorship attestation automatically licenses third-party fonts. Do not commit font files.
